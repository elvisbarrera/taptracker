Frequently asked questions

Do stands need batteries or charging?

No. NFC chips are powered by the customer’s phone when it touches the stand.

Can I use stands and cards together?

Yes. Many businesses put a stand at the register and cards on tables or with staff. Both point to the same Google review page.

Can I change where the stand points later?

Yes. If your Google listing changes, contact us and we update the link.
