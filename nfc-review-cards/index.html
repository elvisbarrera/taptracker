Why most happy customers never leave a review

Most customers who loved their visit mean to leave a review. The problem is the steps: open Google, search for your business, find the reviews tab, tap “Write a review”, sign in. By the time they get home, the moment is gone.

An NFC review card removes every one of those steps. The card is programmed with the direct link to your Google review form, built from your business’s Google Place ID, so the customer lands exactly where they need to be.

How a Tap Tracker review card works

  1. We find your Google Place ID. Send us your business name and address and we look it up for you.
  2. We program and test every card. Your direct review link goes on the NFC chip, and each card is tested on iPhone and Android before it ships.
  3. You place the cards where customers pause. Tables, the register, the front desk, the waiting room or with your staff.
  4. Your customer taps and reviews. The review box opens with your business already selected. They pick the stars, write a few words and post.

Where review cards work best

Cards are pocket size, so they go where a stand would be in the way:

  • Restaurants and bars: on each table, with the check, or carried by servers.
  • Barbershops and salons: at each station, so the client taps while they check the mirror.
  • Clinics, dentists and spas: at checkout, when the visit is fresh.
  • Shops and service businesses: at the counter or handed over with the receipt.

Frequently asked questions

Does the customer need an app?

No. iPhones from the iPhone XS and newer, and most Android phones with NFC turned on, read the card with the phone unlocked. The review page opens in the browser.

Will it work with my Google Business Profile?

Yes. The card opens the review form of the Google listing you give us. If you have more than one location, each location gets its own cards.

Can I offer a discount for a review?

No. Google’s policies don’t allow rewarding or filtering reviews, and we never do it. The card simply makes leaving an honest review easy.
