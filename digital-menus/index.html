Digital menus · NFC & QR · English and Spanish

Digital menus your guests open with a tap

A digital menu with photos of every item, organized by category, that opens when a guest taps an NFC card or scans the QR code on the table. Change a price or an item in seconds, without reprinting.

Why restaurants switch to a digital menu

  • No reprinting when a price, a wine or a special changes.
  • Photos sell. Guests see the cocktail or the dessert before they order.
  • Two languages on the same menu, English and Spanish.
  • Works on any phone, iPhone or Android, with no app.

Open with NFC or QR

Each table gets a card with an NFC chip and a QR code. Newer phones tap; any phone with a camera scans. Both open the same menu.

Can I update the menu myself?

Yes. You update items and prices from your phone, and the change shows up right away.

Does it replace printed menus completely?

It can, or you can keep a few printed menus for guests who prefer paper.
