Review tracking dashboard · Google Sheets · Telegram

Know who on your team brings in the reviews

Every tap on your Tap Tracker cards and stands is logged with the time, the team member and the spot. A live dashboard built on your own Google Sheet shows taps by person, by day and by location, and your phone buzzes when a review lands.

Every tap lands in your Google Sheet

Each card or stand gets a name: a server, a stylist, Table 12, the front desk. When a customer taps it, a new row is added to a Google Sheet that belongs to you, with the time, the person and the place. Open it, filter it, download it. Your data is yours.

A dashboard your managers will actually use

  • Taps by person so you know who to celebrate and who to coach.
  • Taps by day to see which shifts and promotions drive reviews.
  • Taps by spot to learn whether the table, the register or the host stand works best.

Phone alerts on Telegram

Get a notification on iPhone or Android through Telegram:

  • New Google review: the stars and the first words of the review.
  • Every tap: which team member, which table or spot, and what time.
  • Daily summary at close: taps, new reviews and the top team member of the day.

Alerts let you thank the right person during the same shift, while it still matters.

Built on tools you already know

The dashboard runs on Google Sheets and automations built with n8n, and alerts are delivered through Telegram. Pair it with review cards for each team member and a stand at the counter.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to set anything up?

No. We connect the cards, the Google Sheet, the dashboard and the alerts for you.

Who owns the data?

You do. The tap log lives in a Google Sheet in your account.

Can I track more than one location?

Yes. Each location gets its own cards and shows up separately in the dashboard.
