Privacy policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Tap Tracker (“we”) runs taptrackertx.com. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Contact and order forms: your name, business name, email, phone, the services you are interested in and any message you write.
  • Tap data for customers who use our dashboard: the time a card or stand was tapped and the team member or spot it belongs to. We do not collect the name or phone number of the person who taps.

How we use it

  • To reply to your request, prepare a quote and deliver your order.
  • To run the tap dashboard and alerts for businesses that sign up for it.

We do not sell or rent your information.

Who processes it

Form submissions are received through our hosting provider, Netlify, and delivered to our email. Dashboard data is stored in Google Sheets in the customer’s own account, and alerts are sent through Telegram. Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

Google reviews

Our cards and stands only open your Google review page. Reviews are written and published by your customers on Google and are governed by Google’s policies.

Your choices
