Inventory app for bars, wine cellars and restaurants

Bar and restaurant inventory in minutes, not hours

Count with the phone you already have. Point it at a bottle, take a photo of your handwritten count sheet, or type the number in. Everything lands in the same inventory, with separate counts for storage and the bar.

Three ways to count

  • Scan the bottle. Your phone reads the text on the label and finds the product.
  • Photograph your count sheet. AI reads each handwritten line and adds the quantities.
  • Type it in. Fast manual entry when you are adding just a few bottles.

Built for real service

Separate counts for the cellar or storage room and the bar, ready to move into Google Sheets for ordering and cost reports. It was built for and is used at Brasão Brazilian Steakhouse.

Try the live demo.

Who it is for

  • Bars and restaurants that count liquor, wine and beer every week.
  • Wine cellars with hundreds of labels and vintages.
  • Managers who still count on paper and type it into a spreadsheet later. Snap the sheet instead.

Counting faster means counting more often, which means fewer surprises when you place orders and a clearer picture of what is selling. Add digital menus so price changes reach guests the same day.
